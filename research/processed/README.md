# processed/

Source material that has completed the Extraction/Verification/Cross-check/Classification steps of `research/EVIDENCE_INTAKE_PROCESS.md`, but whose final disposition (`verified/` vs. `rejected/`) hasn't been decided yet, or whose extraction output (the per-item findings, before they're folded into a specification update) needs to be kept alongside the source itself.

Once a Decision Matrix outcome (Section 5 of `EVIDENCE_INTAKE_PROCESS.md`) is reached, move the source to `research/verified/` or `research/rejected/` and log the outcome in `research/evidence_log.md`. Nothing should sit in `processed/` indefinitely — it's a working stage, not a final resting place.
